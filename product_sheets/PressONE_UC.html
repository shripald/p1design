Six licenses. Pick the seats that fit each role.

Most teams mix and match: a couple of Advanced seats for power users, Simple for the warehouse phones, a stack of Call Center agents on the support floor, and a Supervisor to keep an eye on everyone. Site Managers pay nothing extra — they're a scope on top of any seat.

01

Office Manager / Site Manager

For IT & ops leads

Admin scope covering the whole domain or a specific geographic site — manages users, devices, caller ID, queues, telephone numbers, and pulls company-wide reporting.

Cradle-to-grave reporting · Custom 911 · Listen / Barge / Whisper
02

Simple

For shared phones & floor stations

The lean seat — common-area handsets, lobby phones, kitchen lines. Calls in, calls out, paging, transfers, voicemail-to-email. No bells, no whistles.

Calls · Transfers · Paging · VM-to-email
03

Basic

For most regular employees

The everyday seat — calling plus the productivity layer: chat, video, SNAPmobile, MoH, conferencing, call recording, screen pop, hot desking.

+ Mobile / desktop app · Video · Recording
04

Advanced

For sales, exec, & customer-facing

The flagship seat — everything in Basic plus Teams integration, SMS/MMS, voicemail transcription, SNAP.HD meetings & webinars, OneFAX, deep CRM/ERP integration, full REST API.

+ MS Teams · SMS/MMS · Webinars · CRM integrations
05

Call Center

For contact-center agents

Built for queue-driven work. Agent web phone, queue routing (round-robin, tiered, ring-all, linear cascade), wrap-up time, statuses, SMS queueing, real-time stats.

Queue routing · Agent web phone · SMS queues
06

Call Center Supervisor

For team leads & floor managers

Everything in Call Center plus the oversight layer — wallboards, custom KPIs, DNIS stats, queue thresholds, agent prioritization, recording controls, real-time analytics.

Wallboards · DNIS stats · Thresholds · Analytics
