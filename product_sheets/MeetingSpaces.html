Meeting Spaces · What's inside

Four products. One platform.

Most teams come for one thing — usually meetings or webinars — and stay for the others. Everything below is included in the platform; what changes between plans is who can host, how many can attend, and which premium features unlock.

HD Video Meetings

Crystal-clear video, screen sharing, online whiteboard, annotation, breakout rooms, file transfer. No download — runs in any modern browser.

Audio Conferencing + AI Transcripts

Toll-free numbers in 50+ countries. Cue™ AI generates transcripts with speaker ID, timestamps, and action-item summaries. Sentiment analysis on every call.

Live Streaming

Stream straight to YouTube or your own audience with HD quality and custom branding. From a 50-person all-hands to a 50,000-person global event.

Webinars

Custom registration forms, speaker spotlight, live polls, Q&A, post-event analytics, shareable recordings. Cross-device, no install for attendees.

Built for compliance.

HIPAA + GDPR compliant transport. PIN-less entry with secure access codes. WebRTC-based — no plugins, no aging desktop client to update across your fleet.
