How we rate audio

Premium

Acoustic Shield plus a second noise layer: Noise Proof (T58W, T58W Pro, VP59) or AI noise cancellation (T77U).

Enhanced

Acoustic Shield, Smart Noise Filtering and a hearing-aid-compatible HD handset. All T4, most T5 and T7. T7 adds a 3W speaker.

HD voice

HD handset and speaker with Smart Noise Filtering. The whole T3 series.

Tiers group the audio features Yealink publishes for each model. Every phone on this page supports HD wideband calls.

Built in vs. dongle

  • Built inWi-Fi or Bluetooth is inside the phone. Nothing to plug in, and the USB port stays free for a headset or recording.
  • Via dongleThe phone supports it through a Yealink USB adapter (WF40/WF50 for Wi-Fi, BT40/BT41/BT51/BT52 for Bluetooth), bought separately.
  • Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.0Only the T7 W and LTE models have the newest radios. The T44W and T5 wireless models use dual-band Wi-Fi 5 (802.11ac) or Wi-Fi 4 (802.11n) and Bluetooth 4.2.
  • Cordless optionsT53, T53W, T54W, T57W and VP59 pair up to four DECT handsets with the DD10K dongle.
