PressONE Agents · What they do

An AI that sounds like your best receptionist.

We don't sell you a generic auto-attendant. We engineer an AI agent that knows your business — your services, your hours, your prices, your booking rules, your CRM. It picks up on the first ring, holds a natural conversation, and gets the caller what they came for.

Books appointments

Checks your real calendar (Google, M365, athenahealth, etc.), offers slots, books, sends confirmations & reminders.

Qualifies leads

Asks the same questions your SDR would. Logs everything to the CRM. Hands warm leads to a human.

Answers account questions

Looks up balance, order status, ticket status, prescription refills — whatever the system of record knows, the agent can read.

Routes intelligently

Recognizes intent in the caller's own words. Routes to the right department — or to a specific person — based on the conversation.

Sounds like a person

Custom voice persona — your brand's tone of voice, your accent, your phrasing. Handles interruptions and back-and-forth without breaking.

Hands off cleanly

When a human is needed, the agent warm-transfers with full context — the human picks up already knowing the situation.

Built around your systems.

Agents plug into the tools you already use — so they're useful from day one, not a year from now.

How we build · Get Started

From kickoff to live agent in 4–6 weeks.

01

Discovery (week 1)

We listen to recordings of your current call flow, sit with your front desk, and map every conversation type to an intent. You approve the scope before we build.

02

Persona & scripts (week 2)

We craft a voice, name, and conversational style. You review and edit. We bake in the disclaimers, compliance language, and escalation rules your industry requires.

03

Integration build (weeks 2–4)

We connect to your EHR, CRM, calendar, ticketing — whatever's in scope. You review test calls weekly.

04

Pilot & tune (week 5)

Soft launch on a single number or a single intent. We listen to every call, refine, and expand scope until the agent is operating at your bar.

05

Go-live & managed support (week 6+)

Cutover. We monitor performance, tune monthly, and add new intents as your business changes. You always have a real PressONE engineer on speed-dial.
