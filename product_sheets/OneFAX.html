OneFAX · How it works

Four ways in. One delivery promise.

Fax remains the highest-trust transmission method for sensitive documents in healthcare, legal, finance, and government. OneFAX delivers it over the modern internet — and meets you wherever your team already works.

Email-to-fax

Compose a fax like a regular email. Attach a PDF, send it to the recipient's fax number @ your OneFAX domain, hit send. Done.

Fax-to-email

Incoming faxes land as PDFs in any mailbox or distribution list you choose. Threaded, searchable, archivable.

Web portal

Browser-based send/receive with cover pages, contacts, fax history, retry logic, and audit logs. No client install.

Fax-to-fax via ATA

Keep your existing fax machine. Our analog telephone adapter (ATA) bridges it to OneFAX over the internet — same wall jack, same workflow, no PSTN line.

Built for compliance-sensitive industries.

OneFAX transports over our secure carrier-grade infrastructure. It meets the workflow requirements teams in healthcare, legal, financial, insurance, and government rely on.

  • HIPAA-aligned transmission with delivery receipts
  • End-to-end audit log of every send and receive
  • Configurable retention & auto-purge
  • Distribution-list routing for incoming faxes
  • Failover routing — never drop a fax on a busy line
