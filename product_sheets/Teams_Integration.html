Teams Integration · Why & what

Teams for chat. PressONE for the phone.

Microsoft Teams is excellent at chat, video, and meetings. But its built-in Calling Plans are expensive, often slower to deploy, and don't come with the support model your team needs. PressONE Teams Integration connects your existing PressONE phone system into Teams as the calling backbone — so you keep the user experience users already know, and PressONE runs the dial-tone.

What this unlocks.

Native calling in Teams

The dial pad, call history, voicemail, and contact list — all live inside the Teams client your users already have open.

Use your existing PressONE numbers

No number ports to Microsoft. No re-printing business cards. Your DIDs stay where they are.

Lower Teams licensing cost

Downgrade users from a Microsoft Calling Plan to Teams Phone Standard. The PressONE seat carries the dial-tone — talk to us for a side-by-side cost comparison against your current bill.

Operator Connect & Direct Routing

Whichever path fits your tenant: we support both Microsoft Operator Connect (1-click admin enablement) and traditional SBC-based Direct Routing.

Call recording & analytics

Compliance-grade call recording inside Teams. Plus the PressONE reporting layer — agent stats, queue stats, cradle-to-grave reporting.

Single support, single vendor

When Teams calling breaks, you call us — not Microsoft. We've already built the runbook for your tenant.

Add PressONE SMS for Teams texting.

Teams doesn't natively text. Pair this with PressONE SMS Pro to send and receive SMS / MMS from inside Teams using the exact same numbers. See the PressONE SMS product sheet for plans and pricing.

Get Started

Most rollouts complete in under two weeks.

01

Tenant readiness check (day 1)

We audit your M365 tenant — Teams Phone licensing, voice routing policies, conditional access. You get a clear go/no-go and license sizing.

Your PressONE DIDs are mapped to Teams users. No porting. No carrier paperwork. No service interruption on existing PressONE devices.

Pilot group (days 4–7)
