The problem

Why your answer rate is dropping.

In 2026, mobile carriers and aggregators (Hiya, First Orion, TNS, Nomorobo, Icehook, TrueSpam, RoboKiller, Truecaller, YouMail) maintain real-time databases of "spam-likely" caller IDs — feeding the carriers' on-screen labels and the federal complaint pipelines at the FCC and FTC. Once your number lands on one — for any reason, fair or not — the carrier surfaces "SPAM Likely" or "Scam Likely" on the recipient's screen. Answer rates collapse. The problem compounds because carriers share lists.
